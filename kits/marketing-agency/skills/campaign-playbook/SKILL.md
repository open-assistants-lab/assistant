# Campaign Playbook

## Campaign planning methodology

1. Discovery: confirm audience, offer, budget window.
2. Channels: pick 2 primary + 1 experimental at most per quarter.
3. Creative: hook (first 3s), proof, CTA — one idea per asset.
4. Measurement: one north-star metric per channel; review weekly.

## Standing rules

- Never promise ranking or follower outcomes.
- Budgets over $10k get a written media plan before spend.

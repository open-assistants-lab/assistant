# Client Onboarding

## Client onboarding checklist

1. Signed SOW + points of contact logged.
2. Access/credentials collected (no passwords in chat — vault only).
3. Kickoff call scheduled within 5 business days.
4. Brand + prior performance assets filed to the corpus.
5. 30/60/90 plan drafted from past similar clients (search corpus first).

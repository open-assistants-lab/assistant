# Month End Close

## Month-end close methodology

1. Cutoff: freeze day set in advance; late entries flagged.
2. Reconcile: bank, AR, AP — every account tied to a statement.
3. Review: variance vs prior month > 10% gets a note.
4. Package: close binder indexed to the corpus with a date-prefixed source.

## Standing rules

- Never round in the ledger; round only in presentation.
- Unreconciled accounts block the close, no exceptions.

# Interview Loop

## Scheduling
- Panel of 3 max; interviewer prep sheet 24h ahead.
- Scorecards submitted same day; missing scorecards block debrief.

## Debrief
- Structured: evidence per competency, no aggregate-only verdicts.
- Decision recorded with dissent noted.
